Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan my notes?

Open a course, go to the Documents tab, and tap the + button to scan a page or upload a file — or use the scan icon inside the AI Tutor or Smart Writer to add a photo directly to what you're working on.

How many free scans do I get?

Free accounts get a daily allowance across the AI Tutor, Quiz, Flashcards, Summary, Smart Writer, and scanning. Your allowance resets every day. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited use.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Revvo → Cancel Subscription. You'll continue to have Premium access until the end of your billing period.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app → go to Settings → Revvo Pro → tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the screen.

Does Revvo work with maths and science?

Yes! Revvo supports LaTeX rendering for complex equations in maths, physics, and chemistry. The AI will automatically format equations correctly.

My scanned notes aren't generating good study material

For best results: ensure good lighting, hold the camera steady, and make sure the text is clearly visible. Printed text works better than very messy handwriting.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → Settings → tap "Delete account". This permanently removes your account and all your data, including documents, notes, and conversations, and cannot be undone.
