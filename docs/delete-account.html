Delete Your Account

Last updated: 23 September 2026

You can permanently delete your Revvo account and all associated data at any time, either from within the app or by requesting deletion here.

Option 1: Delete in the app (immediate)

Open Revvo → Settings → tap "Delete account" → confirm.

This is instant and doesn't require contacting us.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it for you:

Email: abel.aghorighor@suftnet.com

We process email deletion requests within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account (email, password, profile)
  • All courses/modules you created
  • All documents, scanned pages, and notes you uploaded, including their indexed search data
  • All chat conversations with the AI Tutor
  • All quizzes, flashcards, and summaries you generated

This action cannot be undone.

What is retained

We may retain limited records where required by law — for example, transaction records for completed purchases, kept only as long as legally necessary (typically for tax and accounting purposes). Purchase history managed by Apple or Google is governed by their own policies, not ours.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. Cancel it separately through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings to stop future billing.
